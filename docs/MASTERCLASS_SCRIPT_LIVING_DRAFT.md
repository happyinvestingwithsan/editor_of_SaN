## 📌 The 6 Foundational Rules (Recap from Our First Video)

1. **Mistake 1: Trading F&O / Intraday as a Casual Part-Time Job**  
   *The Truth:* F&O is a full-time profession and a big players' game. Believing you can generate easy side-income alongside a 9-to-5 job without years of training and deep emergency reserves drains savings invisibly.
2. **Mistake 2: Price-Based Stop-Loss in Cash Equity Investing**  
   *The Truth:* Stop-loss is essential for leveraged F&O, but it is cancer for cash equity in market leaders. In great companies, a price dip is a discount, not a reason to exit. The fundamental selection process IS your stop-loss, not a 10% price trigger.
3. **Mistake 3: Confusing Price Movement with Business Quality**  
   *The Truth:* A falling stock price does not mean a bad business (e.g. TCS or Asian Paints during negative news cycles). Short-term price follows market sentiment; long-term price follows business earnings. Volatility is our opportunity.
4. **Mistake 4: Over-Concentration (Putting Too Much Money in One Stock)**  
   *The Truth:* Putting 20–30% in one stock creates emotional torture. We strictly cap allocation to protect peace of mind:
   * *Small-Caps:* Max 1.5%
   * *Mid-Caps:* Max 3%
   * *Large-Caps:* Max 5%  
   *(Diversified across 40–50 market leaders so you sleep peacefully).*
5. **Mistake 5: Investing Borrowed Money or Capital Needed Within 3 Years**  
   *The Truth:* Never invest money needed for school fees, weddings, or EMIs in the next 3 years. Never take personal loans or gold loans to invest. Only invest surplus capital so you are never forced to sell at the bottom.
6. **Mistake 6: Trading Without a Defined System (No Fixed Exit / Acting on Tips)**  
   *The Truth:* Buying on tips or emotions without knowing when to sell kills portfolios. Act like a disciplined **Shopkeeper**: know your purchase cost, stick your profit target price sticker on the item, and never compromise your system.

## 🎬 SECTION 1: The Red Signal, The 6 Mistakes & The Retrospective
*(Status: Locked & Aligned)*

### Spoken Teleprompter Text:

Over the last few weeks, you open your portfolio and everything is deep in red. Headlines are screaming, foreign institutions are dumping thousands of crores, and panic is everywhere on social media.

But before we look at any numbers or charts, I want to remind you of a simple rule of the road.

When you are driving—whether you are driving a small Maruti Alto, or an Audi, or a luxury Mercedes Benz—when the traffic light turns RED, what do you do? 

You STOP. You slow down. 

You don't say, *"Hey, I am driving a Mercedes Benz, so I can ignore the red signal and race ahead."* If you break the red signal when the road conditions are bad, you will crash, no matter how powerful your car is.

Right now, the stock market's mood is bad. It is flashing a clear RED light. And when the market is in a bad mood, even the best companies pause. The smart thing to do is not to rush, not to get aggressive, and not to force trades. You slow down, you assess the road, and you let the storm pass.

When we started this channel, we released a foundational video on the **6 Deadly Mistakes that cause 97% of people to lose money in the stock market**:
1. Trading F&O or intraday without treating it as a full-time profession,
2. Putting price-based stop-losses in great companies and getting shaken out by operators,
3. Confusing falling stock prices with bad business quality,
4. Over-concentrating and putting too much money into just one or two stocks,
5. Investing borrowed money or money you need within the next three years, and
6. Trading without a defined system like a shopkeeper who doesn't even know his selling price.

If someone has committed even one of these six mistakes, they cannot sit calm today. Their stress is through the roof. If you haven't watched that video yet, I strongly recommend you watch it. 

But if you have avoided those mistakes and followed disciplined rules, then this video is for you.

Today is not about reacting emotionally to daily market falls. Today is a calm retrospective. We are going to understand what the market is actually trying to teach us, consolidate all the real facts without any fear-mongering, and analyze what risks are still pending ahead so that you carry this learning for every future bear market.

Let us look at what is actually happening in the market.
