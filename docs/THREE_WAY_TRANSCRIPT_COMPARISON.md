## 3. Analysis of Discrepancies and Root Editorial Failures

### 1. Why Did the Rough Cut Retain 5 Bad Takes and Repeats?
The current `analyzer.py` only compares immediate adjacent segments ($i$ and $i+1$) for false starts. When the speaker attempts a thought, pauses for 5 to 15 seconds, and attempts it again (e.g., Take 1 at 75s, Take 2 at 109s, Take 3 at 166s, Take 4 at 173s), these takes are separated by multiple pause segments and filler phrases.
- Because the distance between Take 1 and Take 4 is > 8 segments, the adjacent-pair rule never triggered.
- The paragraph-level retake detector required an exact 4-word identical prefix. In this recording:
  - Take 1: *"I am currently watching 5 stocks including HTFC Bank, Reliance..."*
  - Take 2: *"And as a bonus, I am making the same backtesting tool..."*
  - Take 4: *"I am currently watching 5 stocks including HTFC and Reliance..."*
  The slight lexical variation (*"HTFC Bank, Reliance"* vs *"HTFC and Reliance"*) broke the exact string matching. Consequently, **every single take was treated as unique new content and kept on the timeline**.

### 2. Why Are There Unwanted Pauses and Clipped Words?
- The current pipeline treats **every pause > 1.8s as an independent pause to be shortened to 0.50s**, rather than understanding whether the pause separates an abandoned take from a fresh take.
- When Take 1 was followed by a 10s pause and Take 2 was followed by a 15s pause, the pipeline inserted two 0.50s pauses and joined the failed takes together, creating a surreal sequence where the speaker repeatedly tries and stumbles through the same announcement.
- When cutting pauses, the waveform refiner currently has an asymmetric boundary condition: it prunes 0.25s padding into words, causing word onsets like *"In this tool"* or transitional words like *"For"* to be clipped mid-phoneme.
