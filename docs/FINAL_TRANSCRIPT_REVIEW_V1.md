## 5. Step 4: Specific Review Inquiries & Explicit Recommendations

### 1. Which occurrence of "In this tool" to retain?
- **Recommendation:** **Retain the second occurrence (`39.48s - 40.36s`)** at the start of the framework walkthrough. **Cut the first occurrence (`32.80s - 33.68s`)** at the end of the context sentence.
- **Rationale:** Sentence 2 is completely self-contained and punchy without it: *"I tracked 23 large-cap stocks using a simple 52-week high-low framework."* Sentence 3 benefits enormously from having the visual demonstrative pointer: *"In this tool, when a stock hits its 52 week low, I don't go on immediately buy them."* This replicates the exact cut made in the published Short.

### 2. Should the statement ending around 01:18 be followed immediately by the CTA?
- **Recommendation:** **YES.**
- **Rationale:** The statement ending at 196.48s (*"...understand how the opportunity stands for you"*) is the natural climax of the pitch. The rough-cut candidate failed because it retained an aborted stumble (*"For the full video just go to the YouTube"*) starting at 196.48s. By cutting sharply at 196.90s, the video flows directly and seamlessly into the authoritative CTA (*"For the full video, just go to the YouTube channel and find the latest video."*) across a clean 0.35s–0.45s breath transition.

### 3. Which single "For the full video" version to use?
- **Recommendation:** **Use Take B (`206.30s - 210.48s`) exclusively.**
- **Rationale:** Take A (`196.48s - 199.04s`) is an aborted fragment ending abruptly at *"YouTube"*. Take B is articulate, grammatically complete, and delivered with authoritative vocal projection.

### 4. Is "For your reference, I will attach the link" needed, redundant, or better suited to another position?
- **Recommendation:** **Retain it in its natural recorded position (`210.80s - 212.86s`), immediately following the channel CTA.**
- **Rationale:** The speaker recorded Take B and this bio link sentence as a single continuous breath thought (`206.30s - 212.86s`). It directly fulfills the viewer's immediate question: *"Where do I get the free backtesting tool mentioned 10 seconds ago?"* → *"For your reference, I will attach the link in the bio."* It is not redundant because the previous sentence points to the YouTube channel, while this sentence points to the actual download link.
- **Alternative Consideration:** If an ultra-compressed cut is mandated by the human reviewer, this sentence can be dropped cleanly without compromising grammar. However, our primary recommendation is to **KEEP** it, exactly as the human editor did in the published Short.

### 5. Which sign-off to retain?
- **Recommendation:** **Retain ONLY the final tag (`221.77s - 223.31s`): *"See you in the next one. Happy investing!"***
- **Rationale:** Both earlier occurrences (`213.14s` and `219.99s`) omit the signature tagline *"Happy investing!"*. Retaining only the final tag eliminates the duplicate sign-off loop that plagued the candidate edit.

### 6. Is the ending coherent without any additional phrase?
- **Recommendation:** **YES, 100% coherent.**
- **Rationale:** The sequence:
  > *"For the full video, just go to the YouTube channel and find the latest video. For your reference, I will attach the link in the bio. See you in the next one. Happy investing!"*

```
[Raw Camera Recording]
          │
          ▼
1. Acoustic Transcribe & Word Alignment (GPU Whisper + Waveform Verification)
          │
          ▼
2. Semantic Take Clustering & Competing-Take Identification
          │
          ▼
3. Editorial Cut Recommendation (`FINAL_TRANSCRIPT_REVIEW.md`)
          │
          ▼
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 🛑 MANDATORY HUMAN APPROVAL GATE (Explicit Human Sign-off)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
          │ (Approved Spoken Blocks & In/Out Ranges)
          ▼
4. Deterministic EDL Generation (Strictly binds approved ranges; zero re-decisioning)
          │
          ▼
5. Lossless Video Concatenation & Audio Mix (FFmpeg / NVENC)
          │
          ▼
6. Rendered Transcript & A/V Verification (Independent Whisper audit on output)
          │
          ▼
7. CapCut Project Generation (`draft_content.json` injection)
          │
          ▼
8. Multimodal Visual QA (Contact sheet & subtitle sync inspection)
```

### Core Gate Invariant:
**The rendering and EDL generation engines must be purely deterministic consumers of an approved decision schema.** They must never perform independent retake detection or heuristic pruning after human transcript approval.
